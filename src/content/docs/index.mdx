The **South Asian Canadian Digital Archive (SACDA)** is the digital archive of the
South Asian Studies Institute (SASI) at the University of the Fraser Valley. It is an
[Islandora 2](https://islandora.ca/) repository (Drupal plus Fedora, Solr and a set of
derivative microservices) running in Docker at **[sacda.ufv.ca](https://sacda.ufv.ca/)**.
It replaced an older CollectiveAccess site at `sacda.ca`.

These docs are for the people who keep it running and growing: maintainers, developers,
archivists who ingest collections, and editors who manage pages. They cover the running
system, how content got in, the quirks we hit, and the research that sets the next steps.

## How to read these pages

- **Dates matter.** Numbers and states are dated. The ops pages were snapshotted on
  2026-09-29 and updated with changes through early November 2026. If a page and the
  server disagree, the server wins. Fix the page in a pull request.
- **PROD, TEST and DEV** mean `sacda.ufv.ca`, `sacda-test.ufv.ca` and the developer
  instance. See [Environments and access](/docs/start/environments-and-access/).
- **Open questions** where sources disagreed are collected in
  [Needs review](/docs/reference/needs-review/).
- Every page is also available as Markdown (append `.md`), and the whole site as
  [`llms-full.txt`](/docs/llms-full.txt). See [AI tools](/docs/reference/ai-tools/).
